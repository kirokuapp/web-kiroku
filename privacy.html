Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Welcome to Kiroku (記録) ("Kiroku", "we", "our", or "us"), operated through kirokuapp.com and the Kiroku mobile applications (iOS and Android). Your anime and manga tastes are personal—we do not sell your personal data, display third-party behavioral ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we actually collect, how we use it, which third-party services process it, and how you can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to run Kiroku's tracking, social, and discovery features:

  • Account & Authentication Data: When you register with email and password, we store your email address, chosen username (nickname), and a salted bcrypt password hash. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your verified social identifier (googleId or appleId), email address (including Apple Hide My Email relay addresses), and optional display name if provided by the provider.
  • Profile & Preference Settings: Your selected avatar (from Kiroku's built-in preset anime avatars), optional bio, media type preference (Anime, Manga, or Both), daily episode/chapter goals, notification preferences, and local app preferences (such as dark/light theme and sensitive-cover blur settings).
  • Anime & Manga Library Activity: Your watchlist and reading statuses, episode/chapter progress, rewatch/reread counts, start and finish dates, personal ratings and reviews (1–10), custom and collaborative lists, favorite genres, favorite characters, XP/rank progression, badges, and activity feed entries.
  • Community Interactions: Episode comments, discussion replies, comment reactions (likes/dislikes), list likes, follower/following relationships, blocked user lists, and content moderation reports you submit.
  • Taste Match™ Data: Compatibility scores calculated from your genre affinities, ratings, completed titles, and favorite characters.
  • Optional MyAnimeList (MAL) Integration: If you choose to link your MyAnimeList account via PKCE OAuth 2.0, we store your MAL user ID, MAL username, anime statistics snapshot, and MAL OAuth access/refresh tokens encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM.
  • Push Notification Tokens (Optional): If you enable notifications on a physical device, we store your Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) / Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token and platform (ios or android) to deliver activity alerts.
  • Crash & Error Diagnostics: In production builds, we collect technical error diagnostics (stack traces, app version, request metadata stripped of credentials, and an anonymous internal user UUID) via Sentry to identify bugs and maintain stability.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Based on our codebase and App Privacy disclosures (NSPrivacyTracking: false), Kiroku does not collect or access:

  • Cross-App Tracking or Advertising Identifiers: No ad SDKs, no IDFA tracking, and no third-party behavioral analytics trackers.
  • Payment or Billing Data: Kiroku is free to use with no in-app purchases or paid subscriptions.
  • Sensitive Device Data: No precise or coarse location, no contacts/address book, no health or fitness data, no microphone recording, and no camera or photo library access.

3. How We Store & Use Your Information

  • Core Functionality: To authenticate your account, sync your anime and manga progress, power Taste Match™ compatibility, and display your public profile, lists, and discussions.
  • Device Security: Authentication tokens (accessToken and refreshToken) are stored in encrypted device storage via Expo SecureStore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) on mobile, while non-secret profile metadata and UI settings are cached in AsyncStorage.
  • Transactional Emails: Password reset links and internal moderation alerts are delivered via Resend.
  • Safety & Moderation: To filter profanity, enforce our Zero Tolerance Policy, auto-quarantine content that receives 3 or more reports, and protect users from harassment.

4. Third-Party Services & Integrations

We share only the minimum data required with the following service providers:

  • Apple Sign-In & Google Sign-In: Used solely to verify your identity during authentication. For Apple Sign-In users, we also store a temporary encrypted Apple refresh token in Redis so we can revoke your authorization with Apple if you delete your account.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) & APNs: Used to deliver push notifications you opt into (such as new followers, list likes, comment replies, @mentions, and media recommendations).
  • Sentry: Used for crash reporting and backend/mobile error monitoring. sendDefaultPii is disabled, sensitive fields (passwords, tokens, cookies, emails, authorization headers) are scrubbed before leaving the app, and only your anonymous internal UUID is attached.
  • Resend: Receives your email address solely when you request a password reset.
  • MyAnimeList (MAL): Accessed only if you explicitly connect your MAL account to import or synchronize your anime and manga lists.
  • Anime & Audio Metadata Sources: Public anime, manga, and 30-second theme preview metadata is retrieved from MyAnimeList API, Jikan API, AnimeThemes API, and Apple iTunes Search API without sending your personal account data.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Storage: Our backend runs on PostgreSQL, Redis, and S3-compatible object storage (for avatar assets if custom uploads are enabled, with automatic image metadata stripping).

5. Public vs. Private Information

Other users can view your public profile (username, avatar, rank/level, badges, bio, public lists, ratings, and comments). Your email address, password hash, OAuth tokens, push tokens, and blocked users list are strictly private and never exposed on public endpoints. Users you block are hidden from your feeds, comments, and recommendations, and mutual follows or collaborative list grants are automatically removed.

6. Account & Data Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

You can permanently delete your account and personal data at any time:

  • In-App Self-Serve Deletion: Open Settings → Account → Delete account inside the Kiroku app.
  • Email Deletion Request: If you no longer have the app installed, email support@kirokuapp.com from your registered email address with the subject Kiroku Account Deletion Request.

When you delete your account:

  • Your watchlist/reading entries, ratings, comments, reactions, custom lists, list likes, collaborative list memberships, badges, activities, notifications, favorite characters, genre preferences, push tokens, follows, and blocks are permanently deleted.
  • Your personal identifiers (email, nickname, firstName, lastName, password, googleId, appleId, bio, avatar) and linked MyAnimeList credentials are permanently purged/anonymized.
  • Active sessions are immediately invalidated, any stored avatar files are deleted, and if you signed in with Apple, our servers call Apple's token revocation endpoint (/auth/revoke).

7. Children's Privacy

Kiroku is rated 16+ due to anime/manga themes and user-generated community discussions. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your region). If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at support@kirokuapp.com and we will promptly delete it.

8. Contact & Data Requests

For privacy questions, data access requests, or deletion assistance, contact us at:
