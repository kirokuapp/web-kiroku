Support & Help Center

Need help with Kiroku (記録)? Whether you have a question about your account, MyAnimeList sync, Taste Match™, or want to report abuse or request account deletion, we're here to help.

Contact Support

  • Support Email: support@kirokuapp.com
  • Official Website: https://kirokuapp.com
  • Instagram: @kiroku_app

We aim to respond to all support inquiries within 24–48 hours, and all safety/moderation reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Common Topics & Guides

1. Connecting & Syncing MyAnimeList (MAL)

  • Open Settings → MyAnimeList and tap Connect MyAnimeList.
  • Sign in securely via MyAnimeList's PKCE OAuth 2.0 flow in your browser.
  • Once redirected back to Kiroku, you can tap Re-sync lists or Disconnect anytime from the same Settings section.

2. How Taste Match™ Works

  • Taste Match™ calculates compatibility between fans using your favorite genres, anime/manga ratings, completed titles, and favorite characters.
  • Open any user's profile and tap the Taste Match card to view your synergy breakdown and shareable match card.

3. Reporting Abuse & Blocking Users

  • Report Content or Users: Tap the report option on any comment, rating/review, list, or profile to flag spam, harassment, inappropriate content, or misinformation. Content with 3 or more reports is quarantined immediately and reviewed within 24 hours.
  • Block Users: Block someone directly from their profile, or review your blocked list anytime in Settings → Privacy & Safety → Blocked accounts.

4. Sensitive Content Blur Filter

  • Kiroku enables sensitive cover blurring by default. You can adjust this in Settings → Sensitive Content (+18) → Blur Sensitive Content Images.

5. Push Notifications & Password Resets

  • Manage notification alerts (List likes, New followers, System) in Settings → Notifications.
  • If you registered with email and forgot your password, use Forgot Password on the login screen to receive a reset link via email (valid for 1 hour).
  • If you signed in with Google or Apple, manage your password through your Google or Apple account.

6. Deleting Your Account & Data

  • Inside the App: Go to Settings → Account → Delete account to permanently delete your account, clear your personal data, and revoke linked tokens immediately.
  • Already Uninstalled? Send an email to support@kirokuapp.com from your registered email address with the subject Kiroku Account Deletion Request and we will process it promptly.
